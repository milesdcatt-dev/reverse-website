Biography file

Education, practice, and residential range.

Inson Dubois Wood's biography moves from Cornell architecture and Harvard Graduate School of Design to major residential design offices and his own New York studio. The record is kept clear, direct, and easy to scan.

Harvard GSD

Master of Architecture from the Graduate School of Design at Harvard University.

Cornell

Bachelor of Architecture from Cornell University, with the original biography noting awards and traveling fellowships.

Molyneux

Design director experience with Juan Pablo Molyneux, including residential work and a residential ocean-liner commission.

Easton

Director of design for David Easton, with large-scale projects across the U.S., Europe, and Asia.

2005

Founded his own New York company and continued residential projects across traditional, contemporary, and modern styles.

Rizzoli

The 330-page monograph documents residential work in five countries and gives the studio printed proof.

Homes with memory and structure.

The profile emphasizes family life, respect for each home's existing spirit, and work tailored around the owner's lifestyle. The rooms are meant to feel authored, inhabited, and structurally considered.
