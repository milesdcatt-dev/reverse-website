02
Principal

Architecture first. Decoration with a point of view.

Architect, interior designer, artist, and sole principal — Inson Dubois Wood authors the whole residence under one design eye.

Education

Master of Architecture, Harvard Graduate School of Design. Bachelor of Architecture, Cornell. At Harvard, he studied under Frank Gehry, Rem Koolhaas, Wolf D. Prix of Coop Himmelb(l)au, and Zaha Hadid — graduating at the top of his class with traveling fellowships.

Apprenticeship

Design director for Juan Pablo Molyneux on the residential ocean liner Residensea and on high-end residences in Paris, London, and Naples. Director of design for David Easton, directing 18 large-scale projects across the US, Europe, and Asia.

Studio

Founded his own studio in 2005, based in New York. Work has since included projects in Austria, France, Italy, Japan, Sweden, and Thailand — from Waterfall Mansion in New York to a family home in Greenwich to a villa in Saint-Tropez.

Range

The practice moves between traditional, contemporary, and modern references, often combining styles inside a single residence. Architectural elements define space first; furniture, art, antiques, and material follow.

Philosophy

The work is framed around homes, memory, family life, and an attention to the footprint of each project. Buddhist principles and respect for the spirit of each home guide the design eye, custom-tailored to the owner and lifestyle.
