Pedigree

Not a decorator. A whole architectural weapon.

Inson Dubois Wood holds a Master of Architecture from Harvard Graduate School of Design and a Bachelor of Architecture from Cornell. Before founding his studio, he served as design director for Juan Pablo Molyneux and David Easton, shaping high-end residential work across the U.S., Europe, and Asia.
